Notes for the Exp Team
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Download to a LOCAL disk and point run_test.sh at it with MODEL_DIR=<dir>.
- config.json declares "dtype": "bfloat16" (transformers >= 4.56 naming). On older
  transformers add "torch_dtype": "bfloat16", or it silently loads fp32 and OOMs.
- The GLM-5.2 API key for stages 1, 3 and 4 is sent in a separate e-mail.
